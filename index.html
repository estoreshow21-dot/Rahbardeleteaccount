Delete Your Rahbar Ride Account

You can permanently delete your Rahbar Ride account directly from the Customer App or Driver App.

⚠️ Before you delete your account Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure you no longer need your account or the information associated with it.
👤

Customer Account

  1. Open the Rahbar Ride Customer App.
  2. Open your Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and confirm deletion.
  5. Your account deletion request will be processed by Rahbar Ride.
🚗

Driver Account

  1. Open the Rahbar Ride Driver App.
  2. Open your Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and confirm deletion.
  5. Your account deletion request will be processed by Rahbar Ride.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your Rahbar Ride account/profile is deleted.
  • Personal information associated with your account is deleted.
  • Account-related app data is removed where applicable.
  • You will no longer be able to use the deleted account.
Important: Certain records may need to be retained for a limited period when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, or other legitimate business requirements. Such records will not be used for ordinary account operation after deletion.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or cannot delete your account from the app, contact Rahbar Ride support using the official support contact provided in the app or on the Rahbar Ride website.
